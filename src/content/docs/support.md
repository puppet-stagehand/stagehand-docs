Stagehand is in active pre-1.0 development. The current release is v0.2.3, and each Puppet or OpenVox environment support claim is added only after a maintainer verifies it, so check the [Compatibility Register](/compatibility/) for what is confirmed today.

Premium PCP/orchestrator behavior is unavailable until an approved Stagehand release compatibility record documents it. Do not treat planned integration language as evidence that the behavior ships today.

## Two Channels, Kept Explicit

Route your issue to one of two boundaries, depending on what you're reporting.

### Documentation and Reproducible Public Defects

Use the Stagehand Docs issue tracker for non-sensitive documentation errors, broken public links, and reproducible defects that can be discussed in public.

- [Browse the stagehand-docs repository](https://github.com/puppet-stagehand/stagehand-docs)
- [Review existing public issues](https://github.com/puppet-stagehand/stagehand-docs/issues)
- [Open a public issue](https://github.com/puppet-stagehand/stagehand-docs/issues/new)

Never include credentials, customer data, private diagnostics, or suspected vulnerabilities in a public issue.

### Customer Incidents and Entitled Assistance

For Puppet Core, Puppet Enterprise, PE Advanced, or a customer environment incident, use the private commercial support channel identified by your Puppet agreement or account contact. Include your entitlement and product versions there, after applying your organization's redaction policy.

This public static site does not authenticate customers, determine entitlement, process Forge payments, or create commercial support cases. See [Security and Trust Boundaries](/docs/security/) for how credentials and diagnostics should be handled before you report anything.
