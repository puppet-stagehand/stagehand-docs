<objective>
Close the gap ADR-0003 rule 3 forbids: make the prose agree with the OpenTofu. Two runbooks stop
telling an administrator to hand-craft six least-privilege IAM roles and start telling them to read
six ARNs out of an apply. Three stale sentences elsewhere in the repository stop contradicting
locked decisions.

Purpose: ADR-0003 rule 5 kept the manual provisioning path operative "until the OpenTofu exists".
It now exists. Leaving the manual instruction in place would give an operator two contradictory
procedures for the account's most privileged CI identities. And leaving
`github-environments.md`'s scoping promise untouched while the apply role grants five CloudFront
creates on an unrestricted resource would recreate exactly the prose-versus-reality drift the ADR
exists to prevent — in the direction that flatters the implementation.

Output: both operations runbooks rewritten in place, ADR-0002's stale References pointer closed, and
the design specification and implementation plan reconciled with the locked decisions and the
working tree.
</objective>

the `infrastructure_plan_role_arns` and `infrastructure_apply_role_arns` outputs, and that the

**Keep** the trust-subject bullet list and the two scoping paragraphs. ADR-0003 rule 3 makes them the

Amend the apply-role scoping paragraph — the one currently promising scoping by known ARNs, hosted
zone, resource-name prefixes, and tag conditions. Follow that paragraph's existing structure: state
the scoping that applies, then state the exception and why. Three corrections are required and each
must be stated plainly rather than softened.

First, the tag-condition clause. The sentence promises scoping by the two mandatory tags, hedged by a
trailing qualifier about the conditions AWS supports. The resource-tag condition-key family is
supported by zero CloudFront, ACM, Route 53, and IAM actions, and by no S3 bucket action — so that
qualifier is not a caveat narrowing a mostly-true promise, it is the whole sentence: the tag-scoping
clause resolves to nothing at all. Delete the tag-scoping clause **and its trailing qualifier
together**; leaving the hedge in place while removing the claim just relocates the over-promise.
Replace it with what the implementation actually uses as its least-privilege levers: exact ARNs,
resource-name prefixes, the hosted-zone ARN, and two service-specific condition keys. Name those two
keys by their exact IAM condition-key names in backticks, not by description — `acm:DomainNames` on
the certificate request, and `route53:ChangeResourceRecordSetsNormalizedRecordNames` together with
`route53:ChangeResourceRecordSetsRecordTypes` on record changes. A reviewer checking the policy
against this paragraph must be able to match key name to key name; a prose paraphrase cannot be
checked and is how the current sentence drifted in the first place.

Second, the residual unscoped surface. Name the five actions explicitly:
`cloudfront:CreateDistribution`, `cloudfront:CreateCachePolicy`,
`cloudfront:CreateResponseHeadersPolicy`, `cloudfront:CreateOriginAccessControl`, and
`cloudfront:CreateFunction`. State that AWS defines no IAM resource type for them, so they are
granted on an unrestricted resource, and that this is a limitation of the service rather than a
choice. Do not describe it as temporary or as a future enhancement — nothing in this repository can
change it.

Third, the compensating controls for that residual. Name all three, and name them as controls that
remain in force rather than as reassurance: bootstrap is applied by a human and by no CI job;
CODEOWNERS review is required on `/infra/`; and a second administrator reviews the trust and
permission policies before the ARNs are stored. These are the same three controls ADR-0003 rule 4
already requires, and they are the reason the residual is acceptable.

This paragraph is a visible narrowing of a documented security promise, and it is what user decision
D-A directs: amend the runbook and state the gap plainly. Narrow the claim to what AWS permits; do
not delete the commitment, do not soften it into an aspiration, and do not leave the unscoped set
unnamed. An operator reading it must finish knowing exactly which five actions are unscoped and
exactly what stands in their place. Per D-A, do not attempt to recover the promise by adding
create-time tag conditions to those five actions, and do not split the apply role.
